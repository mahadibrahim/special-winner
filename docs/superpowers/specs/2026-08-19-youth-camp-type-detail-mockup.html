The day, hour by hour. all times placeholder — owner tunes

Every school’s-out day runs the same shape, so kids know the rhythm by mid-morning of day one.

8:45 – 9:00Drop-off & arrival games
Coaches run low-key games as kids land — nobody stands around waiting for the day to start.
9:00 – 10:30Morning session
The coached block — curriculum work in small groups, matched to age and level.
10:30 – 10:45Snack break
From their bag — pack two snacks.
10:45 – 12:00Games that use the morning
Small-sided play that puts the morning’s work straight into a game.
12:00 – 12:45Lunch & downtime
Packed lunch. Kids who need quiet get it; kids who don’t, don’t.
12:45 – 2:15Afternoon session
Second coached block — different focus from the morning, same small groups.
2:15 – 3:00The tournament
The day ends on the thing they’ll talk about in the car.
3:00 – 3:15Pick-up
On time, every time — we know the day doesn’t end when yours does.
