# Youth Training Pathway — Naming Decision

**Date:** 2026-08-17
**Status:** Locked, with two open items noted below
**Scope:** Names for the youth **training** products (classes + camps). Leagues are a
separate section and are **not** renamed by this.

## The system

House-brand prefix with a descriptor per band. Every product, sport, tier and
location takes the same grammar: `Aspire <thing>`.

| Band | Name | Roughly | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Aspire Micros** | 18mo – 3 | Parent-and-child class. The grown-up is on the floor. |
| 2 | **Aspire Minis** | 3 – 5 | First drop-off class. |
| 3 | **Aspire Juniors** | 5 – 8 | First real skills. |
| 4 | **Aspire Academy** | 8 – 12 | Training gets serious. |
| 5 | **Aspire Select** | 8 – 19 | **Invited track, not an age band** — see open item 1. |

The same grammar extends without new naming work: `Aspire Leagues`,
`Aspire Camps`, `Aspire Futsal`, `Aspire Basketball`, `Aspire Worthington`.

## Why this system

Every alternative we developed asked the business to fund two or three separate
brands (Acorn Club + The Forge, Homegrown + Rondo, and so on). The house system
funds **one**, and every mention of every product pays into it. For a pre-launch
business planning more locations and more sports, that outweighed distinctiveness.

**What it deliberately gives up:** the "La Masia effect." Nobody comes *through*
Aspire Juniors — a descriptor names a category, not a place or a pedigree. That
was accepted as the right trade for now; pedigree can be layered on later, once
there are graduates to point at.

## Names rejected, and why

Recorded so they aren't re-proposed:

- **Tots** — SportsOhio runs *KinderTots* ~25 minutes away in Dublin. Live local conflict.
- **Kickers** — SportsOhio again (*KinderKickers*).
- **Cubs**, **Rookies** — existing Lil' Kickers tier names, same age bands.
- **Sprouts** — the most copied name in the category.
- **Littles** — usable, but carries an unrelated adult-subculture meaning that makes it
  a noisy search term for a toddler program.
- **First Team** — the standard football term for a senior squad. Effectively
  **unregistrable**; do not build on it.
- **Homegrown** — descriptive *and* MLS's term of art for an academy-raised player.
  Highest trademark risk of anything considered.
- **Rondo**, **Cantera** — authentic football language, but both need translating for a
  parent. Ruled out on the "must be understood on sight" test that Lil' Kickers and
  Skills Institute pass.
- **Forge**, **Anvil**, **Crucible** — metalwork; saturated in the fitness category and
  it broke the brand world.
- **Academy**, **Elite**, **Select** *standing alone* — category words nobody can own.
  Acceptable **only** behind the `Aspire` house mark, which is what makes them
  defensible here.

## Open items

**1. Is `Aspire Select` an age band or a track?** Four rungs describe an age; Academy
and Select describe a status. "Select" means *selected* at every other Ohio club, so as
a fifth age band it implies a tryout and tells a parent whose kid isn't picked that
nothing exists for their age. **Recommended reading: Select spans ages 8–19 as the
invited track**, sitting across Academy rather than above it. That also gives the
Director of Coaching a genuinely selective group. Not yet confirmed by the owner.

**2. `Micros` is locked but not loved.** The owner accepted it to keep moving. Cheapest
possible thing to revisit — it's copy and a constant, not architecture. The input most
likely to settle it is the Director of Coaching's own vocabulary for that age group,
which nobody has asked him for yet.

## Trademark

Not cleared. `Aspire` + descriptor is a **house-mark family**, which is the strongest
practical position available here — the individual descriptors are weak marks on their
own, and the house mark is what carries protection. Before any spend on signage, kit
or paid media this needs a real clearance search by a trademark attorney; nothing in
this document is a legal opinion.
