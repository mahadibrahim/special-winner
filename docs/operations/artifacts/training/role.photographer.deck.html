Safety & escalation

  • If Photographer no-show, Event Lead escalates to Venue Manager; pull from standby pool or accept reduced media coverage.
  • If Photographer unreachable, Event Lead pings Director; publish authority can be re-assigned for that batch only.
  • If upload fails, Photographer notifies Event Lead who escalates to Director; raw cards are retained until upload succeeds.

You may need to escalate to: Director, Event Lead, Photographer, Venue Manager
