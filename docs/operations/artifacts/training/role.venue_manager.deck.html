Field condition photo (pregame)

You own this

When: About 30 minutes before first kickoff on each surface

If something goes wrong: If Venue Manager unreachable, Facilities captures the photo and escalates to Director per the standard handoff ladder.

Your lead will walk you through this step by step during your first shift.

Rainout decision

You own this

When: Weather/field condition within 2 hours of kickoff suggests cancellation

If something goes wrong: If Venue Manager unreachable, escalate to Director who has unilateral authority to call the event.

Your lead will walk you through this step by step during your first shift.

Your day: in game

  • Incident response (in-the-moment) — You own this

Incident response (in-the-moment)

You own this

When: Incident observed or reported during the match

If something goes wrong: Any life-threatening incident escalates immediately to 911 and Director; Venue Manager remains accountable for documentation but Event Lead may capture the initial form.

Your lead will walk you through this step by step during your first shift.

Your day: post game

  • Incident report finalization — You own this

Incident report finalization

You own this

When: After the incident is stable and the affected match is over

If something goes wrong: If Venue Manager unreachable, Event Lead drafts and Director finalizes; nothing closes until the report is signed off.

Your lead will walk you through this step by step during your first shift.

Facility close walkthrough

You own this

When: After the last match concludes and spectators have cleared

There's a checklist for this — see the checklist slides.

If something goes wrong: If Venue Manager unreachable, escalate to Director per the standard handoff ladder; closure cannot be completed without a walkthrough sign-off.

Your lead will walk you through this step by step during your first shift.

Facility lock and alarm

You own this

When: After the close walkthrough is signed off

There's a checklist for this — see the checklist slides.

If something goes wrong: If Venue Manager unreachable, escalate to Director per the standard handoff ladder; alarm code rotation policy is enforced centrally.

Your lead will walk you through this step by step during your first shift.

Staff clock-out

You own this

When: As each staff member finishes their last task

If something goes wrong: If Venue Manager unreachable, Event Lead witnesses the clock-out and escalates to Director per the standard handoff ladder.

Your lead will walk you through this step by step during your first shift.

Staff debrief

You own this

When: After facility close walkthrough is signed off

If something goes wrong: If Venue Manager unreachable, Event Lead runs the debrief and escalates to Director per the standard handoff ladder.

Your lead will walk you through this step by step during your first shift.
