§ Fine print
  • Dense catalog, lean launch. The grid above is the full draft roster (~18 adult divisions/session, plus youth + futsal). Season 1 opens only the 1–3 divisions you can credibly fill; the rest stay draft and flip open as teams commit, season over season. Fill ramps; the structure exists from day one.
  • Downtown is the bottleneck. One field caps it at ~5 leagues/session, so you trade off Coed depth vs. a Men’s night — and it will waitlist before Worthington does. That makes the downtown second field (or a partner site) the clearest capacity-expansion lever.
  • Futsal runs leagues most nights. The Worthington court hosts 5v5 futsal leagues Mon–Thu (lower team fee, shorter games) with youth academy stacked into the 4:30–6p afternoon slot, then pickup Fri–Sun. Downtown has no futsal court.
  • Drop League — the Man v Fat model. Weight-loss soccer where teams score for goals and collective weight lost. Seeded one night/week (Downtown Sunday early evening, fitting the men’s-professional audience); expandable to a Worthington weeknight as it grows. A genuine differentiator no Columbus competitor runs.
  • Pickup fills the gaps and the late hours. Open to midnight means leagues hold prime time (to ~10:20p) and the 10:20p–12a window becomes a nightly pay-per-session pickup wave; Fri–Sun evenings are pickup-led rather than dead rental slots. Pickup is drop-in (pay-per-session), not a seeded season — it’s scheduled capacity, not catalog rows.
  • Capacity is illustrative. Division-to-night assignments and slot times are a sensible default, not fixed; real assignment follows registered team counts. Confirmed surfaces: Worthington 2 turf + 1 futsal; Downtown 1 turf.
  • 7-game seasons (+ playoff/finals week). Matches the Columbus market — Resolute charges $1,050 for 7 games; Plex runs "7 games + playoffs." Better per-game margin than 8 at the same price, and it resolves the Winter 1 squeeze: Nov 9–Jan 17 yields exactly 7 game-weeks after the Thanksgiving + Dec 21–Jan 3 byes, so no need to play Thanksgiving week. Playoffs are top-team only, so not every team plays week 8.
  • School calendars verified (2026-13). Winter break is universal across all Columbus-metro districts: Dec 21, 2026 – Jan 1, 2027 (back Jan 4/5) — the winter bye + camp week. Thanksgiving: all off Wed–Fri Nov 25–27; Columbus City off the full week Nov 23–27. Spring break SPLITS — early cluster (Westerville/Hilliard) Mar 22–26, late cluster (Dublin/Olentangy/New Albany) Mar 29–Apr 2 — so Winter 2 ends Sat Mar 20 (before the earliest spring break Mon Mar 22), and the Spring start of Apr 5 is confirmed. Spring camp needs two weeks (Mar 22–26 + Mar 29–Apr 2) or defaults to Mar 29–Apr 2 for widest coverage. Still to confirm: Worthington's own PDF (Westerville used as proxy). See docs/research/2026-06-13-columbus-school-calendars-2026-27.md.
  • Seed scale: ~18 adult + youth/futsal divisions × 4 sessions ≈ 70–80 draft seasons. All gated at status=draft; flipping to open publishes per division, any time, reversible.
