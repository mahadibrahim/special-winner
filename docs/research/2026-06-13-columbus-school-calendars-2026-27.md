# Columbus-area 2026-27 school calendars — bye & camp week verification

**Date:** 2026-06-13
**Purpose:** Lock Aspire's 2026-27 indoor season bye weeks and break-week camp dates against the real published district calendars.
**Method:** Deep-research workflow — 16 sources (board-approved district PDFs + calendar pages), 73 claims, top 25 adversarially verified, 24 confirmed / 1 refuted.
**Districts verified:** Columbus City, Westerville, Dublin, Olentangy, Hilliard, New Albany-Plain, Upper Arlington (Thanksgiving only). Worthington's board-approved "Option D" PDF was fetched but its specific dates weren't isolated as surviving claims — Westerville (adjacent) is the verified proxy; **pull Worthington's PDF to confirm before final lock.**

## The three breaks

### Winter break — universal (Dec 21, 2026 → Jan 1, 2027) *(3-0)*
Every district closes Mon Dec 21, 2026 through Fri Jan 1, 2027. First day back: **Jan 4** (Westerville, Columbus City, New Albany) or **Jan 5** (Dublin, Olentangy, Hilliard). The Dec 21–Jan 3 winter bye assumption is **confirmed**.

### Thanksgiving — Wed-Fri everywhere, full week at Columbus City *(3-0)*
All districts off **Wed-Fri Nov 25-27, 2026**. Columbus City closes the **entire week Nov 23-27** ("Wellness Week"). Mon Nov 23 / Tue Nov 24 are school days in every district except Columbus City. Thanksgiving bye assumption is **confirmed**.

### Spring break — SPLITS into two clusters *(3-0)* — the key constraint
| Cluster | Dates | Districts |
|---|---|---|
| **Early** | Mar 22-26, 2027 | Westerville, Hilliard |
| **Late** | Mar 29-Apr 2, 2027 | Dublin, Olentangy, New Albany-Plain |
| **Straddle** | Mar 26-Apr 2, 2027 | Columbus City |

No single week has every district on break. **Earliest spring break starts Mon Mar 22, 2027** — this is the binding constraint on the Winter 2 end date.

### First day of school: Aug 18-20, 2026 *(Westerville Aug 18 confirmed 3-0; others corroborated)*
Well before the Sep 14 Fall start — no first-week conflict.

## Camp windows (maximize districts on break)

1. **Thanksgiving camp:** Mon-Wed **Nov 23-25, 2026** (Columbus City off all three; suburbs off Wed Nov 25). Widest cross-district overlap before the holiday.
2. **Winter camp:** week of **Dec 28, 2026 – Jan 1, 2027** (all districts closed) — optionally also Dec 21-23.
3. **Spring camp — split problem.** No single week serves everyone. Options:
   - **Two weeks:** Mar 22-26 (early cluster) *and* Mar 29-Apr 2 (late cluster) — full coverage if capacity allows.
   - **One week (default):** **Mar 29-Apr 2** — widest single-week coverage (3 suburban districts + Columbus City partial).

## Other no-school days (weekday classes only, not Saturday leagues)
MLK Mon **Jan 18, 2027** (off in several districts — note: this is also the assumed Winter 2 *start*; adult weeknight leagues fine, weekday youth classes pause). Scattered conference/PD days (Dublin Nov 3, Olentangy Nov 30) affect only weekday after-school programming.
