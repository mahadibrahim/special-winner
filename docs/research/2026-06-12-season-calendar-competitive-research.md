# Season calendar competitive research — fall 2026 through spring 2027

**Date:** 2026-06-12
**Purpose:** Reverse-engineer the full-year season catalog (Fall, Winter 1, Winter 2, Spring through 2027) from established indoor soccer operators, to build Aspire's catalog now and gate customer display in the admin.
**Method:** Deep-research workflow — 5 search angles, 16 sources fetched live, 75 claims extracted, top 25 adversarially verified (3-vote panels). 24 confirmed, 1 refuted. All operator facts are self-published web pages fetched 2026-06-12.

**Operators studied:** Arena Sports (Seattle — seed source), Resolute Athletic Complex (Columbus), Stars Indoor Sports (Columbus), SportsOhio (Dublin OH), The Plex (Fort Wayne IN), Maryland SoccerPlex.

---

## 1. Year-round architecture — three viable patterns

Established operators run **5-6 back-to-back sessions per year**, but split the year differently:

| Operator | Pattern | Sessions |
|---|---|---|
| Arena Sports (adult) | **Split winter** | Winter I, Winter II, Spring, Summer, Fall (5) |
| Arena Sports (youth) | Split winter, 3 seasons only | Winter I (late Oct start), Winter II, Spring — Nov-May |
| Resolute (Columbus) | **Single winter** | Fall (Oct-Dec), Winter (Jan-Mar), Spring (Apr-Jun), Summer (Jun-Jul), Early Fall (Aug-Sep) |
| Stars (Columbus) | **Split spring + fall** | Winter, Spring I/II, Summer, Fall I/II — six 8-game sessions |

The "Winter 1 / Winter 2" split is real but not universal — it is one of three proven patterns. *(3-0 verified)*

## 2. How winter splits around the holidays — two models

- **Gap model** (The Plex, youth): Winter 1 Oct 16-Dec 19, Winter 2 Jan 16-Mar 24 — a clean ~4-week dark gap over Christmas/New Year. Each session is 8 weeks delivering 7 games + playoffs, with internal bye weeks (e.g. Thanksgiving). *(3-0)*
- **Overlap/straddle model** (Maryland SoccerPlex, adult): two back-to-back 7-week sessions straddling the calendar turn (Session 1 ~Nov 8-Jan 15, Session 2 ~Jan 3-Feb 26), with a built-in Week 8 makeup week (Dec 27-30) instead of a dark month. *(3-0 / 2-1)*

## 3. Session length and game counts

Market standard: **7-9 weeks, 6-12 games, one game per week**, shortened in outdoor-competing seasons. *(3-0)*

- Arena adult: 12 games standard, **6-8 games Summer/Fall**, one 50-min game/week
- Arena youth: 6-10 games, 45-min games
- Stars: every session exactly 8 games / 8 weeks
- Resolute adult: 7 games/session; SportsOhio summer indoor: 7 games, 24-min halves
- Plex youth: 8 weeks = 7 games + playoffs

## 4. Adult program taxonomy (highly consistent across all operators)

- **Gender:** Men's / Women's / Coed (coed = minimum 2 female field players)
- **Skill tiers:** A ("highest level, very competitive") down to D (rec); Arena adds Beginner
- **Age brackets:** Open / Over-30 / Over-40 (SportsOhio adds O50)
- **Format:** 6v6-7v7 with goalkeeper; SportsOhio plays Open/30+ as 6v6 and 40+/50+ as 7v7
- **Eligibility:** 18+ and not in high school (Arena: 16+ with parent permission)
- **Free agents:** placed into the lowest open divisions (Resolute: Men's Open D or Coed Open D house teams)
*(3-0, five operators)*

## 5. Youth league model — low-commitment, weekend-only, skill-tiered

- Ages 5-18 (U6-U18), **one 45-minute game per weekend, no required practices**, 6-10 games/season
- Arena tiers by competitive level, not just age: **Bronze** (rec/beginner), **Silver** (high-rec/low select), **Gold** (premier/high select)
- Girls typically Saturdays, boys typically Sundays (Arena)
- Plex youth per-team pricing: $420/team U8; $865/team U10-U15; 18-player max
*(3-0)*

## 6. Registration mechanics

- **Close: 10-16 days before session start** across all four dated examples (Stars 11d, SportsOhio 11d, Resolute 16d or at capacity, Plex 10-11d with $20/$100 late fees instead of hard close)
- **Universal $200 non-refundable deposit** at registration, balance due ~first game (Resolute: $100 captain late fee past a dated deadline)
- **Early-bird:** only Stars publishes one — $50 off team fee ~6 weeks before start
- **No operator publishes returning-team priority/rollover mechanics** — likely lives in captain emails/portals
*(3-0)*

## 7. Columbus-market pricing benchmark (summer 2026 snapshots)

| Operator | Team fee | Games | Per-team-per-game |
|---|---|---|---|
| SportsOhio (indoor) | $850 | 7 | ~$121 |
| Stars | $975 (or $110 individual) | 8 | ~$122 |
| Resolute | $1,050 (men's and women's) | 7 | $150 |
| SportsOhio (outdoor) | $975 7v7 / $1,150 11v11 | 8 | $122-144 |

Cluster: **$850-$1,050/team per 7-8-game session, ~$110-$150/team/game, $110 individual**. Youth $420-$865/team by age band (out-of-market Plex). *(3-0; three Columbus-metro operators)*

## 8. Scheduling patterns + summer handoff

- **Adults: fixed weeknights**, 6pm-11:20pm windows; divisions get fixed nights (SportsOhio: Men's Open Thursdays, Co-ed Open Sundays, Co-ed 40+ Fridays)
- **Youth: Saturday/Sunday mornings**
- **Summer:** operators do not go dark indoors — shortened 6-8-game indoor session runs in parallel with a full outdoor season, both starting the week of Memorial Day with a ~mid-May registration deadline (SportsOhio 2026: both start week of May 26, deadline May 15)
- Summer/fall sessions carry tighter payment terms (Arena: paid in full by game 1 vs winter splittable to midseason)
*(3-0)*

## Recommended Aspire 2026-27 calendar skeleton (synthesis — medium confidence)

Fitted to typical Columbus school holidays; follows Aspire's summer 2026 outdoor season 1.

| Session | Dates | Structure | Registration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fall 2026** | Mon Sep 14 - Sun Nov 8 | 8 weeks, 8 games, no holiday conflicts | Opens mid-July; $50 early-bird through Aug 3; closes Sep 3 (11 days out) |
| **Winter 1 2026-27** | Mon Nov 9 - Sun Jan 17 | 8 game-weeks across 10 calendar weeks; byes Thanksgiving week (Nov 23-29) + winter break (Dec 21-Jan 3) | Early-bird Sep 28; closes Oct 29 |
| **Winter 2 2027** | Mon Jan 18 - Sun Mar 21 | 9 weeks: 8 games + playoff week; ends before area spring breaks | Early-bird Dec 7; closes Jan 7 |
| **Spring 2027** | Mon Apr 5 - Sun May 30 | 8 weeks, 8 games | Early-bird Feb 22; closes Mar 25 |
| **→ Summer 2027** | Week of May 31 | Outdoor 7v7 season (Memorial Day-week start, SportsOhio pattern); optional shortened 6-game indoor block | Deadline ~mid-May |

**Program matrix:**

- **Downtown (adult-only):** Coed 7v7 in B/C/D tiers Tue-Thu nights 6-11pm; Men's Open D Mondays; free-agent placement into Coed D; drop-in/pickup Sundays. Add Women's and O30 divisions when demand supports.
- **Worthington:** adult Coed C/D weeknights + youth leagues Sat/Sun mornings (one 45-min game/week, no practices; coed U6/U8/U10/U12 rec-only at launch, add Bronze/Silver/Gold-style tiers later); weekday late-afternoon classes/academy (U4-U8); camps in summer and school-break weeks (Thanksgiving, winter break, spring break — monetize the league bye weeks).

**Pricing:**

- Adult: $950-$1,050/team per 8-game session (top of the Columbus band, matching brand positioning); $120 individual; $200 non-refundable deposit; $50 early-bird 6 weeks out; balance due by game 1
- Youth league: $420/team U8, $750-$865/team U10+ (or ~$110-$140/player)
- Shortened summer indoor: ~25% below standard (6 games vs 8)

## Caveats

- All facts are operator self-published pages (live 2026-06-12); each operator is a single source for its own numbers. Posted prices are summer-2026 snapshots — no operator publishes per-season price variation (Arena varies only payment terms).
- The Plex winter-split evidence is youth-only; its 8-week/7-game structure confirmed only for SELECT divisions (2-1 vote).
- Arena runs 3 youth seasons vs 5 adult seasons — don't conflate youth and adult architectures.
- **Refuted (0-3):** "Resolute limits youth leagues to fall/winter U10-U18" — Resolute's youth scope is unknown; don't cite it as a youth comparable.
- The recommended calendar assumes typical Columbus-area school breaks — not verified against published 2026-27 Columbus City / Worthington district calendars — and does not model Aspire's court capacity.
