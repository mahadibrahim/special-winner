## Findings

### Critical

1. **`skillsDeveloped` is absent on 99 of 104 activities.** Only 4 soccer +
   1 basketball activities carry the array; the field is optional so
   `validateRegistry` never flags it. Skill→activity linkage — the basis for
   "which activities develop this skill" recommendations — effectively
   doesn't exist for ~95% of the catalog.
2. **Competitive (13–15) and refinement (16–18) stages are empty for every
   sport and every content type** — skills, activities, session plans, and
   guidance all zero. The top third of the platform's stated age range has
   no curriculum.

### Important

3. **Physical + psychological domains vanish above fundamentals.** Every
   PH/PS cell in soccer/basketball skill-building and development is 0 —
   exactly where the development stage's own philosophy says "Mental skills
   introduction."
4. **Hockey has full-density fundamentals skills (13, all four domains) but
   zero activities and zero session plans** — skill targets with no delivery
   content. Widest skill-to-delivery gap of any sport.
5. **Baseball is a stub**: 1 skill, nothing else (300-line file vs soccer's
   6,099).
6. **Four elimination-format games are stage-tagged `fundamentals` but
   contradict that stage's keyPrinciples** ("All players touch equipment
   constantly", "Equal playing time"): soccer `world-cup-game`,
   `world-cup-v2` (its own makeEasier lists the failure modes, but
   elimination remains the default script), `musical-balls`; basketball
   `bump-out`.
7. **Six skills are missing pillar fields.** Three soccer skills shipped
   comprehensiveGuide-only with no description/progressions/coaching fields
   (`dribbling-with-speed`, `creating-passing-angles`, `enjoyment-of-play`
   — a known artifact of the upgrade-batch fold); three more lack only
   `description` (`pick-and-roll-ball-handler`, `coordination`,
   `throwing-mechanics`). Soccer `coachability` has an empty
   `commonMistakes`.
8. **Ten basketball guides are structurally thinner** — missing
   `ageExpectations` + `bestAssessedIn` sub-sections (62% coverage vs 100%
   for soccer/hockey/baseball).
9. **Activity mis-/under-tagging in the 5 activities that DO carry
   `skillsDeveloped`**: basketball `layup-lines` tags only
   `passing-basketball` for what is a layup-technique drill; soccer
   `1v1-to-goal` omits `1v1-dribbling-moves`; `rondo-4v1-v2` omits
   `passing-short`/`support-play` named in its own description.

### Minor

10. Discovery stage (3–5) has no sport content anywhere (3 generic prompts +
    1 resource only). Lower urgency given the 2027 youth timeline, but it's
    part of the stated stage model.
11. Eight soccer skill-building/development skills carry 3 coaching
    tips/mistakes/behaviors vs the fundamentals-stage standard of 5.
12. Skill taxonomy thins ~72–80% per stage above fundamentals while
    activities grow — activity `skillsDeveloped` backfill (finding 1) will
    lean on a shrinking skill pool at higher stages.

### Clean

- Progression monotonicity: full-corpus adjacent-level similarity scan found
  zero restated levels; 9 hand-read skills (incl. all consolidation
  survivors) all monotonic.
- The 2026-07-04 shooting consolidation reads coherently; no dangling
  references.
- Product staleness: pilot book (`soccer-fundamentals-6-8.astro`) provenance
  `fa23c93` = current content SHA. The 15 minibooks are hand-authored
  (pre-pipeline, no provenance expected). `pdfs/` gitignored, no tracked
  binaries.
