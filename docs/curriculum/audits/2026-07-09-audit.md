# Curriculum & Coaching Experience Audit — 2026-07-09

Scope: this audit covers (A) the curriculum content registry per the standard
audit checklist, and (B) the product surfaces that deliver it — coach portal,
parent dashboard, and the APIs behind them — reviewed for the experience
coaches, parents, and kids actually get. Six parallel read-only reviews:
coverage, depth, semantic health, product staleness, coach UX, parent/kid UX,
API correctness. `validateRegistry()` passes with 0 structural violations.

## Findings — content (A)

### Critical

- **`activitySuggestions` is a validator blind spot with live broken and
  cross-sport references.** `validateRegistry()` never checks session-plan
  `activitySuggestions`. Found: `4v4-to-small-goals` referenced 8× across 5
  soccer plans (real slug `4v4-small-goals`); `dynamic-stretching-lines` in 3
  soccer plans but the slug exists only as a **basketball** activity;
  `world-cup` and `free-play` in soccer "Ball Mastery Fun Session" don't
  exist at all. Latent today (practice-planner drops the field when applying
  a template), but anything that resolves it breaks or shows a basketball
  drill in a soccer plan. Compounding: hockey and legacy soccer plans use
  free-text display names in the same field while newer plans use slugs —
  the field can't be reliably resolved until normalized.
- **Heading activity violates its own skill's age gate and US Soccer rules.**
  `heading-progression` (soccer/activities.ts) is tagged
  `appropriateStages: ["skill-building", …]` (ages 9–10) and includes jump
  headers, while the skill it develops (`heading-defensive`) is
  development-stage, `introductionAge: 11`. US Soccer prohibits heading in
  training for ages 10 and under. Drop the skill-building tag.

### Important

- **14 of 22 session plans are mostly missing per-segment `coachingScript`**
  (spec: "Script the Coaching" is mandatory per segment). Hockey's 4 plans
  are fully scripted (wave-2 quality benchmark); worst offenders are
  basketball (Ball Handling Development, Defense Development, Transition
  Offense, Advanced Shooting: 0 scripted segments) and soccer Finishing
  Session (0/6).
- **5 plans' segment durations don't sum to `durationMinutes`:** soccer
  Attacking Combinations (67≠75) and Defending Principles (70≠75);
  basketball Team Offense Basics (70≠75), Transition Offense (83≠90),
  Advanced Shooting (83≠90).
- **Internal/editorial voice leaks into coach-facing copy.** Baseball skill
  descriptions carry provenance notes ("One of USA Baseball LTAD's four core
  motor competencies… we introduce it second in our own teaching sequence");
  hockey descriptions reference "USA Hockey ADM small-area-game staples" and
  "reworked to our elimination-free default"; variation text in
  `musical-balls`, `king-of-the-ring`, `musical-basketballs` says "this
  activity is fundamentals-tagged" — internal tagging jargon shown to coaches.
- **`hockey::competitiveness` (fundamentals, ages 6–8) contradicts stage
  principles.** Rubric L3 "battles hard… hates losing", L5 "refuses to
  lose", tip "'Win the battle!'" vs the stage's "Celebrate improvement, not
  results" / "Fun is the #1 priority".
- **Stage-skew suggestions:** basketball fundamentals plan "Shooting
  Fundamentals" suggests `3v3-half-court` (skill-building/development);
  soccer "Attacking Combinations" (skill-building) suggests
  `combination-play-circuit` (development-only); `ball-handling-circuit`
  (fundamentals, 6–8) claims to develop `crossover-dribble`
  (development-stage, intro age 11).

### Minor

- Rubric ceilings not age-calibrated on some fundamentals skills
  (`shooting-jump-shot` L5 "unlimited range", `vertical-jump` L5 "dominates
  above rim", soccer `shooting` L5 "elite finishing") — L4/L5 become dead
  levels for 6–8s. Baseball's "for their age group" phrasing is the pattern
  to copy.
- `commonMistakes` semantics drift on refined skills (reassurances/warning
  signs rather than mistakes) — fine content, wrong label if UI renders
  "Common Mistakes".
- `assessmentActivities` present on only 3/77 skill guides; activity
  `diagram` 12/124 and activity-level `comprehensiveGuide` 14/124
  (acknowledged v2 partial coverage).
- `introductionAge` missing on 6 skills (`dribbling-with-speed`,
  `creating-passing-angles`, `enjoyment-of-play`, `pick-and-roll-ball-handler`,
  `coordination`, `throwing-mechanics`).

### Depth & tone (positive)

All 77 skills pass every depth check: populated `comprehensiveGuide`
(median ~5.3k chars, no duplication), 5 distinct progression levels that
genuinely escalate, populated `coachingTips`, full guide substructure
(levelDetails ×5, ageExpectations, redFlags, parentExplanation,
homeActivities) on 77/77. Zero stub/placeholder text anywhere. Elimination
games are defused with nobody-sits-out defaults. Coaching scripts and
guidance prompts align with stage principles and are warm, kid-appropriate,
jargon-free.

### Product staleness

- Current content SHA: `8ca773b8`. The only provenance-stamped product,
  `src/pages/books/soccer-fundamentals-6-8.astro` (`fa23c93`), is **15
  content commits stale** — generated pre-wave-2, so it likely still carries
  content the refinery deliberately corrected.
- **15 of 16 minibooks under `src/data/minibooks/` have no provenance header
  at all** — staleness is unverifiable for ~94% of the product catalog.
  Regenerate/stamp, and add stamping to the minibook generation path.

## Findings — product experience (B)

Full detail lives in the review transcripts; this is the ranked digest.

### Critical — a coach or parent hits a broken/blank/false experience

1. **`/coach/standings` is a dead page** — `StandingsTable` is mounted with
   no props (`standings.astro:18`), nothing sets `isLoading` false →
   infinite spinner; on fetch failure it renders fabricated mock standings.
   Linked from dashboard Quick Actions.
2. **`/coach/schedule` shows fake games as real** — coaches with no
   scheduled games (or on fetch error) see hardcoded `mockGames`
   ("Dublin FC"…) with no indication; and `handleSaveScore` swallows API
   failure and updates local state anyway — score entry falsely "succeeds"
   and vanishes on reload (`coach-schedule.tsx:482, 511-519`).
3. **"New Assessment" is a dead end from the player detail page and the
   nudge-card deep link** — `sportId` is never passed, so the form's team
   list is empty, `/api/coach/skills` is never called, the coach sees "No
   skills found — Try adjusting your filters", and submit silently no-ops
   (`coach-assessments-overview.tsx:498`, `assessment-nudge-card.tsx:88`,
   `player-assessment-detail.tsx:240-244`). Breaks the main entry to the
   assess→snapshot→radar loop.
4. **Session detail blanks the whole page on any action failure** — the
   `error` state used by save/status/reflection/delete handlers triggers the
   full-page error view, discarding loaded state and typed reflection text
   (`session-detail.tsx:250` + handlers). Should be `toast.error`.
5. **Assessment pipeline integrity (API):** `POST /api/coach/assessments`
   never validates `seasonId` against the coach's org/teams; snapshot
   recompute failures are swallowed with a 201 (radar silently stops
   updating); assessment→snapshot→summary runs without a transaction; and
   `player_skill_summary` lacks a unique index on
   (family_member_id, skill_id) — concurrent assessments create duplicate
   rows and the unordered read-then-upsert then picks arbitrarily forever.
6. **Cross-tenant curriculum reads:** `GET /api/coach/skills` (any
   authenticated user, no coach check, no org scope — full progressions/
   rubrics cross-tenant), `GET /api/coach/activities` and
   `GET /api/coach/templates` (no `organizationId` filter; admin
   counterparts scope correctly).
7. **Parent dashboard "Coach Notes" is permanently mock-empty**
   (`coach-notes.tsx:36-38` hardcodes `[]`, "real data wiring pending")
   while real coach note text exists in `playerAssessments.notes`; its
   "View All" button is dead, and `/dashboard/notes` is an orphaned all-mock
   page with dead Print/Export buttons. The "Development Progress" expander
   on `children-overview.tsx:336` likewise hardcodes `skillAssessments: []`
   — an interactive control permanently wired to nothing.
8. **Development report renders raw enums and a broken icon map** — API
   omits `slug`, component keys `DOMAIN_CONFIG[data.domain.slug]`
   (undefined → all four domains identical blue icon) and headings show raw
   "technical"/"psychological" while the radar above shows "Technical"
   (`domain-progress-card.tsx:164,192`, `reports/[familyMemberId].ts:193-199`).
9. **Radar contradicts the cards** — snapshots skip season-less assessments
   (`snapshots.ts:86-98`) and the radar needs 3 domains, so a genuinely
   assessed kid can see "No assessments yet" above populated domain cards.
10. **Co-parents and adult self-members are locked out of development data**
    — reports/achievements/family-member endpoints check only
    `parentUserId === user.id`, ignoring the `family_member_parents` join
    table and the self-XOR-parent model (adult players can never see their
    own report).

### Important

- Practice planner: "Save Session Plan" vs "Save as Draft" both create
  drafts (`status` never sent; API hardcodes draft) — and "Start Session"
  requires `planned`, so the CTA never appears without manual status flips.
  Related: `canStart` also excludes past-scheduled sessions — exactly when a
  coach opens the plan at the field.
- Dead controls: "View all assessments", "Add Player Note" quick action,
  pre-practice checklist "Start Practice" (no `onComplete` passed), parent
  "Export Report"/"Share Progress", achievements "View All" deep link lands
  on the wrong tab (`?tab=` never read).
- Orphaned built components (dead code): `player-notes-editor.tsx` (376
  lines; roster shows "coming soon" toast instead), `in-session-reminder.tsx`,
  `skill-selector.tsx`, `CoachingTipsList`, `skill-progress-chart.tsx`,
  `achievement-gallery.tsx`.
- Swallowed fetch errors masquerade as empty states (assessment form skills
  fetch, resource library, attendance tracker) — "No skills found" when the
  request actually failed.
- Convention drift: most coach/parent surfaces hand-roll spinner/error/empty
  UI instead of `LoadingSkeleton`/`ErrorBanner`/`EmptyState`/`toast.error`
  (`coach-message-composer.tsx`, `coach-teams.tsx`, `child-profile.tsx` are
  the compliant reference implementations). Attendance tracker also uses
  off-system gray/green palette instead of cream tokens.
- Coach API hygiene: attendance never verifies `rosterId` belongs to
  `teamId`; bulk attendance delete-then-insert not transactional; PUT
  attendance has no zod; standings update races on concurrent completion
  PUTs (non-atomic read-modify-write); session save re-increments activity
  `usageCount` on every edit; `templateId`/`activityId` accepted by bare id
  cross-tenant; prompts/resources POSTs insert raw JSON; several coach reads
  gate on session only (parents can enumerate coach guidance); unclamped
  `?limit=` params 500 on junk.
- Parent-visible tone: coaches' free-form `notes` shown to parents verbatim
  with no "parent-visible" hint on the coach form, while the growth-framed
  `strengths`/`areasForImprovement` fields are collected but never surfaced.
  Radar has no legend (solid vs dashed, rings = levels); "Understanding
  Levels" explainer exists only on `development.astro`, not the Progress tab.
- Mobile: score entry is only reachable via a hover-revealed pencil;
  segment edit/delete are `opacity-0 group-hover`; drag handle affordance
  with no DnD implemented; 32px tap targets in attendance rows.
- Public guides: floating "Back to Coach Portal" CTA on all four
  parent-pitched guides bounces parents off role-gated `/coach`; unglossed
  jargon (ELM, TDEQ-5, PCA Double-Goal) on the parent-facing index.

### Minor

- Dangling "Soccer • " when division null; "vs TBD" placeholders; dark-theme
  leftovers on cream surfaces; raw lowercase status badges; duplicate empty
  states on the dev report; red `TrendingDown` as the sharpest element on a
  kid's progress page; 404-vs-403 existence-oracle inconsistency;
  vacuous post-merge e2e specs asserting `body` visibility on nonexistent
  coach routes (`tests/e2e/coach-dashboard.spec.ts:139-253`).
