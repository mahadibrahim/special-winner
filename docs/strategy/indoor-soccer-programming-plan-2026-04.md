# Indoor Soccer Partnership — Programming Plan

**Maximizing utilization across two facilities, four fields, year-round.**

Drafted 2026-04-27. Companion to `indoor-soccer-pricing-strategy-2026-04.md`.

---

## 1. Capacity baseline

Assumptions: 2 fields per facility (4 total). Operating hours 8am–11pm = 15 hours/day × 7 days = **105 hours/week per field**, or **210 field-hours/week per facility**, or **420 field-hours/week total**.

Realistic Year 1 paid utilization target: **55%** (~230 paid field-hours/week). Year 2 target: **70%** (~295 paid field-hours/week). Empty hours go to retail single rentals via the facility's own booking — not Aspire's problem.

The two facilities have **completely different demand curves**. Programming each independently is the entire point of a programming partner.

## 4. Cross-facility programming concepts

A few products work BETTER spanning both locations:

### Aspire All-Access Pass
**$79/month** — Drop-in pickup at either facility, plus 20% off any league registration, plus priority tournament entry. Drives loyalty, smooths revenue, marketed as the "Aspire member" tier.

### Cross-suburb travel teams
Worthington-trained youth competitive teams enter tournaments at downtown (and other Columbus venues) — turns suburban training into competitive output. Aspire becomes a club, not just a league.

### Coach development pipeline
Use OSU student-athletes (downtown facility access) as coaches/refs at Worthington. Pays students, provides skilled labor, builds relationships.

### Two-location season passes
Adult player in Powell who wants Express weeknights but Standard weekends? One profile, both facilities, easy switching. Stars and Soccer First can't offer this.

## 6. Seasonality

| Period | Worthington focus | Downtown focus | Tournament tempo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sep–Nov (Fall)** | Youth fall league (3 sessions/wk peak) | Adult fall league launch | 2 Halloween-themed events |
| **Dec (Holiday)** | Holiday clinics, no-school camps | Reduced league play, 1-day social tournaments | "Turkey Bowl" Thanksgiving + "Holiday Cup" |
| **Jan–Mar (Winter prime)** | Peak youth indoor (no outdoor option) | Peak adult indoor + corporate league season | "MLK Cup", "Presidents Day Classic", "March Madness" |
| **Apr–May (Spring)** | Spring youth league + outdoor crossover | Adult spring league | "Spring Slam" tournament series |
| **Jun–Aug (Summer)** | Summer camps daily 9am–4pm; reduced league | Adult summer league + heavy pickup | "Summer Jam" tournaments |

Summer is the lowest field-utilization season (everyone wants outdoor). Counter with **summer day camps** — half-day or full-day camps at $250–$450/week, runs 9am–4pm M–F, gobbles field-hours that would otherwise sit empty.

**Summer camps revenue potential** (Worthington only, 8 weeks × 30 kids × $325 avg) = **~$78K** in a season that would otherwise be dead.

## 7. Operational requirements

### Staffing
- **Worthington tournament/event director** (PT, 20 hrs/wk peak season) — $25/hr
- **Downtown tournament/event director** (PT, 25 hrs/wk peak season) — $25/hr
- **Refs pool** — 12–15 active refs across both facilities, $25 youth / $35 adult per game
- **Skills/academy coaches** — 4–6 at Worthington, paid $40–60/hr instruction
- **Camp counselors (summer)** — 6–8 at Worthington, $15–18/hr
- **Front desk / check-in** — facility owner provides at both locations (negotiated)

### Tech & operations
- All registration through Aspire platform (no third-party tools)
- Telegram team groups for every league (already shipped per memory)
- Live scoring tablets at field-side for tournaments
- Bracket/schedule monitors on facility walls (vendor: $1.5K one-time per facility)
- Branded banners and field decals (per OA branding rights)
- Photo/media coverage of tournaments (per existing media-Phase-2 strategy)

### Marketing cadence
- **6 weeks before season**: registration opens, email blast to all prior participants
- **4 weeks before**: paid social media push (Powell/Lewis Center geo-targeting for Worthington; OSU/downtown geo-targeting for Downtown)
- **2 weeks before**: registration close, kit orders placed
- **Tournament marketing**: 4 weeks lead time, separate from league marketing
- **Always-on**: drop-in pickup at downtown is marketed weekly (TOCA-style "join anytime")

## 8. What the facility owner gets out of this

This programming density would be impossible for a pure rental operator to build. Specifically:

1. **~245 paid field-hours/week year-round** (vs typical indoor facility 80–120 paid hours/week)
2. **Year-round revenue smoothing** — tournament cadence + summer camps cover the dead months that kill rental-only operators
3. **Brand association** — facility becomes "the Aspire facility," not generic rental space
4. **Customer acquisition for owner's own retail rentals** — every league family becomes aware of the facility and becomes a private rental candidate
5. **Concession / food-truck revenue uplift** — tournament days alone drive thousands in incremental F&B
6. **Reduced operational lift for owner** — Aspire handles registration, scheduling, ref management, customer service, marketing
