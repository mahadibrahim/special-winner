§ STRATEGY

Three independent revenue legs.

No single product carries the summer. A full weeknight adult lineup at Worthington, a community franchise nobody else has, and late-night inventory the rest of the market leaves on the table. Three facilities of demand, only two locations needed.

01

Adult league core.

A full six-night weeknight adult lineup at Worthington. Tiered C/D divisions, age variants (Over 30, Over 40), free-agent placement, every standard format Columbus indoor adult expects. Six nights of league play, $1,050 per team, seven-game season.

  • VenueWorthington

Continental Cup Series.

Liga Latina, Aspire Africa Cup, Aspire Asia Cup — three concurrent community leagues feeding one championship final on August 22–23. Not branding-by-translation. A program built for the calendars these communities already keep.

§ SUMMER DAY CAMPS · WORTHINGTON · 8 WEEKS

Eight weeks. One million minutes of soccer.

Themed-week structure runs Mon Jun 9 – Fri Aug 1, 9am–4pm. Half-day option, sibling discount, scholarship slots reserved on Heritage Week. $325/wk full-day · $195/wk half-day. Field A used during camp hours; B and C remain open for rental.

Week 01
Skills Week
Jun 9–13
Foundation — passing, dribbling, shooting. Sets the technical base for the whole summer.
Week 02
World Cup Week
Jun 16–20
Each kid drafted to a country. Friday World Cup mini-tournament. Tied directly to Continental Cup theme.
Week 03
Goalkeeper Week
Jun 22–26
Specialty week, premium pricing. Position-specific training daily, dedicated GK coach.
Week 04
Star Player Week
Jun 29–Jul 3
Four days (skip July 4). Each day spotlights an international star — Messi, Salah, Mbappé, Vinícius.
Week 05
Speed & Agility
Jul 7–11
Athletic development — sprint mechanics, cutting, change-of-direction. Conditioning paired with ball work.
Week 06
Tournament Prep
Jul 14–18
Friday Night Lights mini-tournament caps the week. Parents invited; medals all around.
Week 07
Heritage Week
Jul 21–25
Latino · African · MENA · European · USA. Cultural food, music, mini-history. Kids share their family's soccer culture.
Week 08
Showcase
Jul 28–Aug 1
Final week. College coaches invited Friday for HS-aged showcase. Demo games, individual highlights.
Why summer camp here works
Day camps convert dead daytime field-hours into ~$106K of revenue and ~$67K of net contribution in a season the rest of the indoor industry treats as overhead-only. Worthington has the family demographic. Field A handles 30+ kids/day comfortably while B and C remain available for facility-direct rentals.
