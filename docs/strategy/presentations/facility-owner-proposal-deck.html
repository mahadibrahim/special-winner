§ THE TWO MARKETS

Different demand. Different playbook.

Worthington · near Powell & Lewis Center

Suburban family destination.

Youth-heavy demand. Premium adult coed evenings. After-school skills clinics. Saturday mornings packed with U6–HS games. Summer day camps when competitors go dark.

What runs here

  • Youth leagues — U6 through HS, Sat AM + weekday eve
  • Skills clinics + academy — afterschool 3–6pm
  • Adult coed Sunday + late-week leagues
  • Walking soccer 50+ · Mom & Tot · Birthday parties
  • Summer day camps Jun–Aug, 9am–4pm
Nearest direct competitor — Stars Indoor (5 min away). Geographic capture from Powell/Lewis Center families is the moat.
Downtown · OSU corridor

Urban adult social hub.

Students, young professionals, downtown employers. Express format weeknights. Drop-in pickup memberships. OSU + Wexner partnerships. Tournament + bar atmosphere.

What runs here

  • Adult Standard leagues weeknight 7–10pm
  • Adult Express (25-min × 4 teams/hr) M–F 5–10pm
  • Drop-in pickup membership ($40/mo unlimited)
  • OSU faculty / Wexner Med staff weekday morning
  • Greek IM cups + downtown corporate leagues
Zero major indoor soccer operator in the OSU corridor. Genuine market whitespace — pricing flexibility AND market creation.

// Partnership Proposal 08 / The deal
§ THE DEAL · REVENUE-SHARING FRAMEWORK

Streams of revenue. How we share them.

StreamProposed framework
Programming field rental Partner-rate pricing across prime-time leagues, off-peak hours, and tournament weekends. Multi-tier structure with specifics worked out together based on what fits your operations. Aspire commits to volume; rates reflect that commitment.
Tournament weekends Revenue share on tournament gross or fixed daily rate per event — your choice each event. Plus tournament sponsorships we source and share with you (proposed: 15–20% of net sponsorship revenue tied to your venues).
F&B + vending revenue You own all base concession revenue. On Aspire event days (tournaments + signature league nights), Aspire receives a modest share (proposed: 10–15%) of incremental F&B — reflecting our atmosphere and event-programming investment, and aligning both sides on driving event-day revenue.
Ancillary, branding + ops Birthday parties + summer camps run by Aspire pay you standard field rental. Aspire installs branding (we cover production cost) and gets free dedicated storage at each location. All operations — registration, scheduling, customer service, marketing — handled by Aspire.

All percentages and minimums proposed for discussion — final terms shaped together during walk-through and term sheet finalization.

// Partnership Proposal 09 / Why this works for you
§ THE FACILITY OWNER CASE

Why this works for you.

01

Idle capacity becomes committed revenue

Year 1 minimum field rental from Aspire ($40K) is more than you'd net filling those same hours via retail — because retail won't actually fill them all. We pay for utilization you're not capturing today.

02

Increased retail demand from awareness

Tournament weekends bring 200+ families through your facility. Many become retail rental customers afterward. Aspire programming is your highest-volume marketing channel.

03

Operational lift removed

League customers complain to Aspire, not to you. Refunds, scheduling conflicts, payment issues, ref no-shows — all our problem. You stay focused on the building.

04

Concessions revenue uplift

If you have concessions at either facility, tournament days will be your highest revenue days of the month. Aspire's atmosphere programming drives traffic that buys.

05

Year-round revenue smoothing

Most indoor facilities die in summer. We anchor summer with day camps, tournaments, and Express leagues that fill during months your competitors go dark. Real money in the dead season.

06

Brand identity for your facilities

Aspire-branded banners, scoreboards, photo walls. Your facilities become destinations for indoor soccer in Columbus, not interchangeable rental space.
