## 1. Opportunity

A facility owner with **two indoor soccer facilities, four fields total** has invited Aspire to be their dedicated programming partner.

- **Facility 1 — Worthington** (near Powell / Lewis Center border). Suburban-family demographic.
- **Facility 2 — Downtown Columbus** (near OSU). Urban / student / young-professional demographic.

Current field rental rate: **$170/hr** (in-market for Columbus; major operators like Stars, RAC, Soccer First all quote rates privately and likely sit in $140–$200/hr range; only Soccer Field Academy publishes a public rate at $135/hr).

The two-facility split is the core strategic asset. A pure rental operator can't capture the value of having both demographics. A programming partner can.

## 4. The density unlock

Standard format: 1 game / field-hour, 2 teams sharing.
**Express format**: 2 games / field-hour, 4 teams sharing (25-min games).

| Facility $/hr | Express field cost (8 games ÷ 4 teams) | Total cost | Margin at $895 | Margin % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $170 (no negotiation) | $340 | $605 | $290 | **32%** ✅ |
| $130 | $260 | $525 | $370 | 41% |
| $120 | $240 | $505 | $390 | 44% |

**At Express-format density, $895/team works profitably even at the facility's full $170 retail rate.** This is the unlock — Aspire can run a price-competitive product without needing rate concessions.

## 7. Facility negotiation — partnership structure, not rate fight

The framing that works: **"Your rate is fair. The challenge is that any programmer running leagues at retail-rate inputs has a hard time hitting market-clearing prices. Let's structure this so we both win on volume and density, not on rate."**

### Structure to propose
| Term | Ask |
|---|---|
| Multi-year exclusive programming partnership | 2–3 years, both facilities |
| Standard rate (prime-time leagues) | $130/hr (vs. $170 retail; reflects volume + scheduling-loss-free booking) |
| Express format slots (4 teams/hr) | $130/hr — owner captures more per field-hour than retail single-team rentals |
| Off-peak rate (M-F 9am–3pm) | $80/hr — incremental revenue from currently-empty inventory |
| Tournament rate (full weekend takeover) | $130/hr blended OR revenue share (15% of tournament gross) |
| Aspire scheduling priority | First right on prime-time blocks before retail release each season |
| Volume guarantee | $40K Year 1 minimum across both facilities; step-up to $65K Year 2 |
| Bar/concession share (downtown) | 10–15% of food/drink driven by Aspire participants |
| Storage + branding rights | Free at both facilities |

### What NOT to give up
- **Pricing control** — Aspire sets registration prices, not the facility owner
- **Customer relationship** — registrations live in the Aspire platform; Aspire owns the family data
- **Kit/merchandise revenue** — fully Aspire's
- **Multi-facility flexibility** — don't commit exclusivity *in the other direction* (Aspire can add additional venues later)
- **Tournament title sponsorships** — Aspire owns sponsorship sales; revenue share with facility on bar only

## 8. Key risks and watchpoints

- **Insurance.** SAM coverage required by OA §9.5(d). Get a quote early — this is real money and will refine the youth model.
- **Roster fill.** Model assumes full 9–10 player teams. Half-empty teams kill per-player math. Set minimum-viable-team policy.
- **Refs.** Suburban Ohio indoor refs are scarce. Confirm a ref pool before committing season schedules. May need to recruit/certify your own.
- **Stars competitive response.** Stars is 5 min from Facility 1. They may match per-player youth ($149) or bundle a kit. Strategy: stay ahead on tech (Telegram, modern platform) and atmosphere (tournament events).
- **Downtown demand uncertainty.** No incumbent means no proven demand. First two seasons should over-invest in marketing to OSU intramural overflow, Greek life, Wexner Med staff, and downtown corporate teams.
- **Kit timing.** Per Momentec strategy memo, kit orders need 3–4 week lead time. Set registration close 5 weeks before season start.
- **Year 1 OA salary ($60K) is fixed overhead.** Need ~40 teams/season to cover salary + shared overhead. The two-facility partnership easily clears this.
