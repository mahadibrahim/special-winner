# Indoor Soccer Tournament Strategy

**Aggressive, brand-defining tournament series with food trucks, party atmosphere, and real margin.**

Drafted 2026-04-27. Companion to `indoor-soccer-pricing-strategy-2026-04.md`.

---

## 1. Strategic thesis

Tournaments are not a side product. They are:
1. **The fastest path to brand recognition.** A great tournament puts 200+ players + their families through the facility in one weekend. Word travels. League sign-ups follow.
2. **The highest-margin product** in the catalog. Per-tournament gross margin is 45–55%, vs. league margins of 30–40%.
3. **The atmosphere/identity creator.** Food trucks, DJ, photo booth, vendor village — this is what differentiates Aspire from the EZFacility-and-fluorescent-lights competition. Stars and Soccer First do not do this.
4. **The off-season anchor.** Summer indoor is dead — tournaments make summer revenue real.
5. **The cross-sell engine.** Every tournament participant is a warm lead for a league. Every spectator is a warm lead for a youth registration.

Target: **24 tournaments per year minimum across both facilities** by end of Year 1. Average **$5,500 net contribution per tournament** = **~$132K/year contribution from tournaments alone.**

## 4. The atmosphere kit — what makes it a "party"

This is what separates Aspire tournaments from a Stars or Soccer First league weekend. Every tournament has a consistent atmosphere package; size scales with event tier.

### Standard atmosphere (all tournaments)
- **Food trucks** — 2–3 trucks per event, partnerships with Columbus Food Truck Association vendors
- **Music** — DJ for premier tournaments ($300–500/day); curated Spotify with a real PA system for smaller events
- **Branded everything** — Aspire banners on every field, scoreboard branding, podium branding, photo backdrop
- **Live bracket display** — large monitors showing brackets, scores, next-up matchups
- **Aspire merch table** — t-shirts, kits, water bottles, hats; staffed
- **Photo booth or branded photo wall** — drives social shares with Aspire watermark
- **Awards podium with trophies** — visible center of facility for medal ceremonies
- **Welcome desk with check-in + atmosphere host** — first impression matters

### Worthington-specific (family events)
- **Inflatables / bounce house zone** ($400–600 rental) — kids who aren't playing have something to do
- **Face painter** ($200/day) — at peak weekends
- **Kids' contest zone** — keepie-uppie contest, fastest-shot radar gun ($100 rental), penalty shootout
- **Family-friendly food trucks** — ice cream, kettle corn, mini-donuts always
- **Goodie bags for U8 and under** — Aspire-branded, $5/bag cost, perceived value $20

### Downtown-specific (adult social events)
- **Beer garden** — partner with mobile beer garden vendor or facility's existing license. This is the single biggest atmosphere/revenue lever downtown.
- **Late-night DJ format** — keep events going until 11pm or later for adult tournaments
- **Sponsored tab / drink tickets** — corporate sponsorships often want this branding
- **Standing-room spectator zones** — make it social, not parents-on-bleachers
- **After-party partnership** — local bar deals "Aspire tournament players get 20% off at [Bar X]" within walking distance

## 5. Food truck operations

### Vendor model
- **Application + curation** via Aspire form — vendors apply, we choose 2–3 per event for variety
- **Fee structure**: $150–250 flat fee per truck per day **PLUS** 5% commission on sales
  - Flat fee is non-negotiable, paid in advance to commit slot
  - Commission tracked via end-of-day register receipts (honor system + spot checks)
- **Power & water**: facility owner provides; this is part of the partnership negotiation
- **Vendor village layout**: 2–3 trucks lined up at facility entrance, picnic tables, signage

### Vendor categories to target
| Category | Why | Examples in Columbus |
|---|---|---|
| Tacos | Universal appeal | Los Guachos, Buckeye Bourbon House taco truck |
| BBQ | Family-friendly | City Barbeque truck, Ray Ray's |
| Pizza | Easy / appeals to kids | Mikey's Late Night Slice mobile |
| Ice cream / dessert | Kid bait, peak summer | Jeni's mobile, Dough Mama mobile |
| Coffee / cold brew | Morning tournament essential | Brioso, Roaming Goat |
| Healthy / smoothies | Athletic crowd | Northstar Cafe truck (if available) |

### Beer garden (downtown only)
- Partner with **mobile beer garden vendor** (e.g., Land-Grant pop-up, Wolf's Ridge mobile)
- They handle license, insurance, staffing
- Aspire takes 15–20% of gross bar sales
- Realistic per-tournament bar take: $4,000–$8,000 gross → $600–$1,600 to Aspire
- Local craft brewery sponsorship potential ("Land-Grant 4v4 Cup")

## 6. Sponsorship strategy

### Sponsorship tiers per tournament

| Tier | Price | What sponsor gets |
|---|---|---|
| **Title sponsor** | $1,500–3,000 | Tournament name ("[Brand] 4v4 Classic"), big banner at every field, MC mentions, social media inclusion, vendor table |
| **Field sponsor** | $500–750 | Branded banner on one field for the day, MC mention, social media inclusion |
| **Award sponsor** | $300–500 | Brand on trophies/awards, MC ceremony mention, table |
| **Vendor table only** | $200 | Table at event, no field branding |

### Sponsor categories to target
- **Local sports retailers** — Columbus Running Company, Front Runner, Snipes
- **Healthcare** — OhioHealth, Mount Carmel, Wexner Medical (downtown), local PT/sports med practices
- **Apparel/equipment** — Momentec/Augusta dealers (per procurement strategy), New Balance Columbus
- **Restaurants/bars** — local establishments wanting facility-area visibility
- **Auto dealers** — minivan-buyer demographic for youth events
- **Real estate agents** — Worthington/Powell agents specifically (family demographic)
- **Insurance** — local agents pay readily for community visibility
- **OSU-adjacent businesses** (downtown only) — student-targeted brands

### Year-round sponsorship packages
For sponsors who want exposure across multiple events:
- **Bronze**: 4-event package, $4,000 (vs. $5,200 á la carte)
- **Silver**: 8-event package, $7,500
- **Gold**: full-year (24 events), $18,000 — title sponsor at 4 chosen events + field sponsor at 12 + table at all
- **Platinum (Aspire Series Title)**: $30,000 — name attaches to entire series ("Aspire presented by [Brand]"), all premier slots, year-round visibility

Annual sponsorship revenue target Year 1: **$30,000+**

## 9. Operational requirements

### Per-tournament checklist (build a runbook from this)
- [ ] 8 weeks out: registration opens, sponsorship outreach begins
- [ ] 6 weeks out: food truck applications close, vendors confirmed
- [ ] 4 weeks out: marketing push (paid social, email blast to all leagues, Telegram broadcast)
- [ ] 3 weeks out: ref scheduling confirmed, equipment rentals booked (DJ, inflatables, monitors)
- [ ] 2 weeks out: registration close, brackets generated, communications to teams
- [ ] 1 week out: final confirmations to vendors/refs/staff, atmosphere kit delivery scheduled
- [ ] Day-of: 6am setup, event execution, real-time bracket management, end-of-event awards
- [ ] Day-after: photo distribution, social posts, immediate "next event" promo to participants

### Staffing requirements per tournament
- 1 tournament director (12–16 hrs day-of)
- 1 atmosphere/welcome host (8 hrs)
- 4–6 referees (per format)
- 1 photographer (per media strategy memo)
- 1 staff for merch/check-in table (8 hrs)

Total tournament staffing cost: ~$1,500–2,000 included in per-tournament budget.

## 10. Risk and contingency

- **No-show teams** — require non-refundable deposit at registration ($100/team). Standard.
- **Weather impacts on attendance** — indoor isn't weather-dependent for play, but sponsor turnout/spectators may be. Build a "rain plan" (= no plan needed) but communicate it as a strength.
- **Food truck cancellation** — always confirm 2 days prior; have a fallback truck list.
- **Sponsor non-pay** — invoice in advance; sponsor tier perks contingent on cleared payment.
- **Ref shortage** — biggest risk. Maintain relationships with at least 20 active refs; pay competitively to retain.
- **Liquor/beer license** (downtown bar partnership) — confirm vendor's license covers facility; have backup vendor.
- **OSU calendar conflicts** — football home games kill downtown adult attendance. Schedule downtown tournaments around the OSU football schedule. Use football Saturdays for "tailgate" themed Sunday events instead.
