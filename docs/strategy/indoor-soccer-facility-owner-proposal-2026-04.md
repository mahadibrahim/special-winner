## What we're proposing — partnership structure

### Term
- **2-year initial term**, with mutual 1-year renewal option
- Exclusive programming partnership: Aspire is the sole league/tournament operator at both facilities. Your retail rental business (private bookings, walk-on field time, your own clinics if any) is unaffected.

### Field rate structure
| Time block | Rate | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Prime-time leagues (M–F 6–10pm, Sat 8am–10pm, Sun 12–9pm) | **$130/hr** | Reflects volume commitment + scheduling-loss-free booking; eliminates the gaps in your retail calendar |
| Express format slots (4 teams sharing a field-hour) | **$130/hr** | You capture more $/field-hour than retail single-team rentals (we pack the field, you collect from us, not from a single $170 booking) |
| Off-peak (M–F 9am–3pm) | **$80/hr** | Fills currently empty daytime inventory — pure incremental revenue |
| Tournament weekends (full-day takeover) | **$130/hr blended** OR 15% revenue share, your choice | Tournaments are your highest-traffic days for ancillary revenue (concessions, etc.) |

Your standard $170/hr retail rate stays intact for non-Aspire walk-up rentals. We're not asking you to discount your headline price. We're proposing a wholesale partnership rate for guaranteed-volume programming.

### Revenue commitment to you
- **Year 1 minimum**: $40,000 in field rental fees from Aspire across both facilities
- **Year 2 minimum**: $65,000 (reflecting league fill maturation)
- **Payment**: weekly invoice based on actual hours used; minimum trues up at year-end if Aspire underbooks

### Concession and ancillary
- **Bar/concession revenue share** (downtown facility, if applicable): you receive 85–90% of bar revenue from Aspire programming attendees, Aspire takes 10–15% as event programming partner
- **Food truck operations**: Aspire manages vendor relationships and collects vendor fees; you provide power/water access at facility
- **Branded signage rights**: Aspire installs banners, scoreboard branding, and field decals at both facilities (we cover production)
- **Free dedicated storage room** at each facility for Aspire equipment

### What Aspire retains
- Pricing control on registration
- Customer relationship and family data (registrations live in the Aspire platform)
- Kit / merchandise sales revenue (separate from facility rental economics)
- Tournament sponsorship sales revenue (our sales effort, our customer relationships)
- Multi-facility flexibility — Aspire may add additional venues elsewhere in Ohio over time

## Why this works for you

**You currently rent fields one hour at a time. Most competitors do too.** The challenge is that retail rentals leave significant idle capacity — particularly weekday daytime, weekend afternoons in some seasons, and holiday weeks. You've also got the operational burden of managing rental customers, scheduling, payment collection, and conflict resolution.

**This proposal converts idle capacity into committed revenue, removes operational lift, and gives your facilities a brand identity that drives further demand.** Specifically:

- **Year 1 minimum revenue from Aspire ($40K)** is more than you'd net from filling those same hours via retail at $170/hr — because retail won't actually fill them all. We're paying for utilization you're not currently capturing.
- **Increased retail demand from awareness.** Tournament weekends alone bring 200+ families through your facility. Many become retail rental customers afterward.
- **Reduced churn and customer-service burden.** League customers complain to Aspire, not to you. Refunds, scheduling conflicts, payment issues — all our problem.
- **Concession uplift.** If you have food/drink at the downtown facility, tournament days will be your highest revenue days of the month. Aspire's atmosphere programming drives traffic that buys.
- **Facility-rental cross-sell.** Birthday parties, private trainings, adult company outings — every Aspire participant becomes a candidate for these higher-margin rentals.
