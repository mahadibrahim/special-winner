## 1. Strategic thesis

Summer is the indoor industry's worst utilization quarter — outdoor competes for everything. The existing programming plan counters with day camps and tournaments. **This summer adds a third counter: community leagues that draw the populations who actually want to play in summer, indoors, in air conditioning, on the formats they grew up on.**

Three of the largest, most soccer-passionate diasporas in Columbus are:

- **Latino / Hispanic** (~6% of metro, concentrated West Side / Hilltop, South Linden, Northland, Whitehall — Mexican largest, then Salvadoran, Guatemalan, Puerto Rican, Honduran)
- **African** — both East African (large Somali, Ghanaian, Nigerian, Eritrean, Ethiopian populations in Westerville / Hilliard / North Linden — Columbus has one of the largest Somali communities in the US) and West African
- **Middle Eastern / MENA** (Iraqi, Syrian, Lebanese, Yemeni, Palestinian, Jordanian, Iranian — concentrated Hilliard / Dublin / Worthington corridor near Noor and surrounding mosques)

All three communities **already play soccer recreationally outdoors all summer**, mostly on cracked rec-park fields with no refs, no schedule reliability, no medical coverage, no photos, and 95°F+ heat. Indoor AC + real refs + real schedule + real social atmosphere is a step-change for them, not a downgrade. **This is a market that wants what we sell, in the season nobody else can sell.**

The "Aspire Continental Cup Series" — three concurrent community leagues feeding a multicultural championship final — is **the brand-defining moment of Year 1.** It positions Aspire as Columbus's home for community soccer, generates outsized social media reach, and gives the venue partner exactly the kind of cultural relevance a pure rental operator can never build.

## 3. The three community leagues — "Aspire Continental Cup Series"

### Branding architecture

Three leagues run concurrently June 1 – Aug 16. Each champion advances to the Continental Cup Final on Aug 22-23. The series mirrors the names of the real continental tournaments the diaspora communities follow — instant recognition.

| League | Cultural framing | Real-world parallel |
|---|---|---|
| **Liga Latina** | Hispanic / Latino community league | Copa América |
| **Aspire Africa Cup** | East + West African community league | AFCON |
| **Aspire Asia Cup** | Middle Eastern + South Asian community league | AFC Asian Cup |

Branding intentionally mirrors continental tournaments these populations grew up watching — names that feel native, not corporate.

### Format and structure

Each league runs **8 regular-season games + 2 playoff weeks = 10 weeks total** (June 1 – Aug 9), with **Continental Final Aug 22-23**. All three use **7v7 Express format** (25-min halves, 4 teams sharing per field-hour) — both because it's the financially-correct unlock at retail facility rates AND because 7v7 is the format these communities play outdoors.

| Element | Default | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Format | 7v7 Express, 25-min halves | Matches outdoor cultural format; field-economics work at $170/hr retail |
| Roster | 10 player minimum, 14 max | Real-world Latino/African outdoor teams run 10-14 |
| Refs | 1 ref per game (USSF certified) | Express format keeps ref cost contained |
| Uniforms | BYO (bring own jersey) for season; Aspire ref-friendly pinnies provided | Critical: avoid kit lead time blocking summer launch |
| Optional kit | $130 slim kit (Momentec) — pre-order only, not required | Per existing procurement strategy |
| Tiebreakers | Goal differential, then head-to-head | Standard |
| Trophy | Continental-themed engraved trophy + championship medals | Designed to evoke the real tournament trophies |

### How 4 leagues fit a 4-hour Worthington weeknight (3 fields)

**Worthington has three fields (A/B/C); Downtown has one field.** Three fields × 4 hours (6–10pm) = 12 game-slots per weeknight. With Year 1 conservative 5–6 team leagues, each league plays ~3 games per night = 4 leagues × 3 = 12 games. **Exactly fits 12 slots.**

```
Mon 6–7pm:   Field A = Coed C   |  Field B = Men's C   |  Field C = Coed C overflow / warm-up
Mon 7–8pm:   Field A = Coed C   |  Field B = Men's C   |  Field C = Men's C overflow
Mon 8–9pm:   Field A = Coed D   |  Field B = Men's D   |  Field C = Coed D
Mon 9–10pm:  Field A = Coed D   |  Field B = Men's D   |  Field C = Men's D
```

Sustainable for Mon/Tue/Wed/Fri (4 leagues each). Thursday (2 leagues) and Sunday (2 leagues) are lighter — those evenings have plenty of leftover capacity for skills clinics, open rental, or community-league overflow. **If a matched league fills to 8 teams (Resolute scale), extend that night to 11pm — the third field gives the headroom.**

### Underfill landing pattern

If a matched league has **fewer than 3 teams** by 2026-05-22 deadline:
1. **Combine C and D** into a single division ("Coed Open Combined") for that night.
2. If still under 3, **combine Over 30 with Open** for that night.
3. If still under 3, **refund or move teams** to the nearest matching night/division.

Resolute uses the same gentle landing — never a hard cancellation that kills future trust.

### Why match exactly

1. **Player preference signaling.** Players know Resolute's lineup. Mirroring tells them "we have your league" with no explanation needed.
2. **Deadline gap is a feature.** Resolute closes May 1; we close May 22. Three-week window catches teams that didn't get into a Resolute league or want a guaranteed-spot backup.
3. **Recruitment narrative simplifies.** "Same day, same format, same price, north Columbus location" is a one-line pitch.
4. **Future divergence is easy.** Once Aspire has its own brand momentum, innovate the format. Year 1, lower friction by mirroring.

### Saturday is left open at Worthington

Resolute uses Saturday for tournaments + open rental. Aspire-Worthington Saturday is **Africa Cup men's + coed** (per §3) plus monthly youth tournaments — so Aspire is *adding* programming on the day Resolute leaves blank. Net effect: matched Mon–Fri/Sun + differentiated Saturday.

## 3.6 Late-night extension — competitive whitespace

**Aspire stays open later than Resolute.** Resolute's last-game slot is ~9–10pm (their final time block on the registration portal). Aspire's facility partnership targets **11pm Worthington / 12am Downtown standard close, with select late-night programming until 1am.** This is hours of inventory Resolute structurally cannot use — and it's exactly the slot the community leagues want.

### Why late-night is the natural fit for community leagues

**Summer Maghrib (Islamic sunset prayer) in Columbus is ~9:15pm in June and July, ~8:45pm in August.** Observant Muslim players in the East African (Africa Cup) and MENA (Asia Cup) communities cannot start games until *after* Maghrib. The 9–11pm window is the *first* slot of their playable evening, not the last. Extending into 11pm–1am gives them a full league night, not a single rushed slot.

**Latino late-night culture is real and economically grounded.** A meaningful share of working-class Latino soccer players in Columbus work restaurant industry / hospitality / overnight shifts. Restaurants close 10–11pm; shift-end is 11pm-midnight; their playable window is 11pm–2am. Resolute closes too early to capture them. Aspire's late-night Liga Latina is the only indoor product they can actually attend.

**Indoor late-night also wins on heat.** Summer Columbus indoor temps with HVAC load: facilities cool down 10pm onward. Players show up, AC is at peak efficiency, no thermal stress. The product is genuinely better at midnight than at 6pm.

### Scheduling rules for late-night

- **Worthington late-night runs Saturdays only as full leagues** (Sat 5pm–1am is Africa + Liga Latina + Africa Late). Mon–Fri Worthington matched-league nights end 10pm so coordinator/ref staff can finish by 10:30pm. Late pickup 10–11pm is a low-staffing drop-in product.

- **Sunday closes at 11pm everywhere** (no league after the existing 10pm matched-Resolute Sunday Coed end).

### Late-night ref + staff pool

- **Late-shift ref premium:** $5/game over standard ($30 youth / $40 adult for late slots). Recruit refs from communities that play in those leagues — culture-aligned refs cost the same and reduce friction.
- **Coordinator on-site close to 1am:** the league coordinator who runs Saturday Africa Cup is the same coordinator who runs Saturday Africa Cup Late and Liga Latina Late. Build their pay around a single Saturday block (5pm–1am) at $25/hr = $200/Saturday.

### Why this matters

A schedule that exceeds field-hours is fiction. The constraint is real:
- **Each Express league needs ~1.5–2 hours per scheduled night** (6-team league × 7 games / 8 weeks ≈ 2.6 games × 30 min = 78 min).
- **Each Standard league needs ~3–5 hours per scheduled night** (6-team league × 7 games / 8 weeks ≈ 2.6 games × 60 min = 156 min).
- **No two leagues can share one field simultaneously** at Downtown — sequencing matters.
- **Three fields at Worthington enable parallel programming**: 4 leagues × 3 games per night × 1 hr = 12 game-slots = exactly 3 fields × 4 hours.

If Year-1 fill **exceeds projection**, response is to extend Worthington weeknights to 11pm (gives 3 more field-hrs/night = 12 incremental field-hrs/wk → can absorb 6 additional leagues at Express format or 2 at Standard format). Downtown headroom is harder — single field caps additive capacity at ~30 incremental field-hrs/wk, which sustains another 4-5 Express leagues on currently-light nights (Tue, Thu).

## 14. Risks and watchpoints

- **Community trust takes time.** Year 1 fill rates for the community leagues will probably be 60–70% of the projected. The strategic value is the brand foothold, not Year 1 revenue. Don't price-cut to fill — let the second season's word-of-mouth do the work.
- **Cultural missteps.** A misspelled country name, a wrong holiday date, an inappropriate music choice at a Final — these get talked about. Every public-facing piece (flyers, social, signage) needs review by the relevant league coordinator before going live. **Build that review step into the workflow.**
- **OSU summer enrollment is half the academic year.** Downtown Asia Cup + Africa Cup Express may underfill in June (graduation/transition); plan for Express format flexibility (4-team/hr can run with 2 fields-of-1 + 1 field-of-2 if needed).
- **July 4 weekend is dead.** Don't run regular league matches that week — only the Friday Express tournament.
- **Heat / venue HVAC.** Indoor AC is the differentiator. If either facility's HVAC fails on a 95°F day, attendance evaporates. Confirm HVAC reliability + backup plan with facility owner during partnership negotiation.
- **Eid date verification.** Eid al-Adha 2026 is calculated as May 27 (lunar). It can shift ±1 day based on moon sighting. Confirm with Noor Islamic Cultural Center the week before.
- **Camp scaling.** Day camp staffing is sized for ~40 kids/week. If demand spikes (heritage angle drives broader market), have a pre-cleared bench of 3 additional counselors.
- **Continental Cup Final attendance.** Conservative spectator estimate: 400 across both days. If it's actually 1,000+, food truck commitments may underdeliver and bathroom capacity at Worthington gets tight. Monitor RSVP signups by Aug 1 and adjust vendor commitments.
- **Photo / media consent.** Some MENA + East African families are sensitive about photos of women / children on social media. Default to opt-in photo consent at registration; never auto-publish without explicit family consent. This matters more than usual for these communities.
- **Refs from inside the communities.** Recruit refs from each diaspora community where possible — game tone changes when refs share cultural context. Pay equal to existing ref pool, no exceptions.
