# Adult 4v4 Flag Football League Guide

## Format & Why 4v4

Flag football is played 4v4 on our boarded indoor fields at Worthington (535 Lakeview Plaza Blvd). The format decision reflects field size: our turf is 110 ft × 60 ft (37 yd × 20 yd), which is significantly larger than the official NFL Blitz indoor 4v4 maximum (25 yd × 20 yd) but smaller than NFL Flag 5v5 regulation (70 yd × 30 yd).

**Why 4v4 works:**
- Every player runs a route on every snap — no linemen, no standing around
- The fully boarded turf field keeps play contained and fast-moving
- Roughly double the touches per player compared to a 6v6 format
- Fast games (two 20-minute running-clock halves) fit multiple matches in a night

## Rules

### The game

- **4v4 flag, no contact** · two 20-min running-clock halves
- **No QB runs** — handoffs, pitches and laterals behind the line only
- **7-second pass clock** (play is dead if the ball isn't out)
- **No diving, no jumping to avoid a flag pull** · spinning allowed
- **Flag pull = down** · ball spotted at the flag

### Coed rules

- **At least 1 female player on the field at all times**
- **Females may sub for males, not vice-versa**
- **No gender restrictions on positions** — anyone can play QB

*Note: The coed rule (minimum 1 female on field) is adjustable before registration opens; confirm with league management before finalizing.*

### Conduct & safety

- **No contact:** no blocking, no stripping, no flag guarding
- **Zero tolerance** — violent conduct = ejection
- **Mouthguards recommended** · flat / turf shoes only, no cleats
- **Flags provided;** shirts must be tucked (flags visible)

### Roster & standings

- **Roster 6–10 (4 to play)** · locks after game 3
- **Standings:** 3 pts win / 1 draw / 0 loss · tiebreak: H2H → point differential
- **Deposit** $200 non-refundable · paid in full by game 1

## Divisions

### Men's 4v4
Open competitive division. Roster 6–10, everyone plays.

### Coed 4v4
At least 1 female player on the field at all times; anyone can play QB. The social-but-real-football option.
