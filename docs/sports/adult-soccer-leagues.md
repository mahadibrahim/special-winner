## Product summary

Co-ed, Men's & Women's **7v7** indoor soccer leagues at **Worthington** and **Downtown** (Columbus, OH).

## Venues & divisions

Two venues. Division offering (from the catalog seed plan, grouped by venue):

- **Worthington — Adult 7v7 (9 divisions):** Coed B, Coed C, Coed D, Coed 30+, Coed 40+, Men's C, Men's D, Men's 30+, Women's Open
- **Downtown — Adult 7v7 (4 divisions):** Coed B, Coed C, Coed D, Men's Open / A
- **Worthington — Futsal 5v5 (4 divisions, optional):** Coed Rec, Men's B, Coed Comp, Men's A

A "division" combines **format** (gender: Coed / Men's / Women's), **skill level** (A–D, or Open = all levels), and optional **age bracket** (30+ / 40+). Each division is a bookable season instance.

## In-game rules (rulebook)

Headline product facts above are canonical. **Detailed in-game rules** (walled-arena
restarts, coed minimums, conduct/cards, standings, mercy rule, equipment) live in the
research-derived rulebook: `docs/research/2026-06-12-league-rules-research.md` —
authoritative **except** the roster size and season/playoff structure, which this
document overrides. Summary of the rules to surface on the site:

- **The game:** 7v7 incl. keeper · two 24-min running-clock halves · no offside · all restarts direct within 5s · no GK punts (→ free kick at top of arc) · free substitution on the fly · walled-arena rules (three-line violation, boarding, play-off-the-wall, ceiling restart, GK throw-in from arc).
- **Coed:** min. 2 female field players (1 to start); keeper gender-neutral; females may sub for males, not vice-versa; macho/safety rule.
- **Conduct & safety:** no slide tackling (GK exception in box); penalty box yellow = 2 min, red = 5 min + 1-game suspension; zero tolerance for violent conduct; shin guards required, no cleats indoors (flat/turf only).
- **Roster & standings:** roster **up to 14 (7 to play)**, locks after game 3; 3 pts win / 1 draw / 0 loss; tiebreak H2H → goal differential → fewest conceded → most wins → most scored; mercy rule caps recorded differential at 5; $200 non-refundable deposit, paid in full by game 1.
