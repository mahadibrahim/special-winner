## U8 Co-ed: Discovery Stage

### Development Focus
- **Primary Goal:** Fall in love with lacrosse
- **Philosophy:** Level playing field, fun for all

### Skills to Develop

| Category | Skill | Description |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| **Stick work** | Cradling | Basic ball control |
| | Ground balls | Scoop and protect |
| | Passing | Basic catch and throw |
| **Movement** | Running with stick | Coordination |
| | Dodging | Basic moves |
| **Shooting** | Basic shot | Introduction |

### Practice Structure (45 minutes)

| Segment | Duration | Content |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Warm-up | 5 min | Running, dynamic movement |
| Stick skills | 15 min | Cradling, ground balls |
| Passing/catching | 10 min | Partner work |
| Games | 10 min | Small-sided play |
| Scrimmage | 5 min | 7v7 |

### What TO DO
- Use youth-sized sticks
- Soft balls for beginners
- High activity, low wait time
- Focus on fun and movement
- Rotate positions frequently

### What NOT TO DO
- ❌ Body contact
- ❌ Position specialization
- ❌ Complex plays
- ❌ Score tracking/standings
- ❌ Long explanations

## Assessment Benchmarks

### Stick Skills

| Skill | Emerging | Developing | Competent | Advanced |
|-------|----------|------------|-----------|----------|
| Cradling | Ball falls out | Maintains in motion | Both hands | Under pressure |
| Catching | 40% | 60% | 80% | 95% |
| Ground balls | Hesitant | Competes | Wins share | Dominates |
| Shooting | Misses cage | On cage | Accurate | Placement |

### Game Skills

| Skill | Emerging | Developing | Competent | Advanced |
|-------|----------|------------|-----------|----------|
| Dodging | Runs into defense | Creates space | Beats defender | Sets up others |
| Defense | Out of position | In position | Makes plays | Controls |
| Field IQ | Lost | Aware | Anticipates | Quarterbacking |

## Key Safety Guidelines

| Topic | Guideline |
|-------|-----------|
| **Equipment** | Properly fitted helmet, gloves, pads |
| **Contact rules** | Strictly enforced by age |
| **Stick checks** | Legal technique only |
| **Goalie protection** | Crease rules enforced |
| **Hydration** | Regular water breaks |
| **Heat** | Modified practice in extreme heat |
