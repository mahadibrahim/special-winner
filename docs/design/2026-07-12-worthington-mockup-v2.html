NEW · ANSWERS THE REST OF THE QUESTIONS

Good to Know

What do I wear?

Turf shoes or firm-ground cleats and shinguards on the fields; flat-soled shoes on the futsal courts. No metal studs.

Can I come alone?

Yes — register solo for any division ($120) and we'll place you on a team, or just drop in for pickup any session.

Is there parking?

Plenty — a big free lot right outside the door at Lakeview Plaza. You will not circle the block.

Can people watch?

Plenty of spectator seating and standing room. Kids welcome with an adult.

What about kids' programs?

Youth leagues run Saturday and Sunday mornings now. Classes, academies, and camps launch in 2027 — join the list to hear first.

How late are you open?

Doors open at 4pm; league slots run 6–11pm and open play regularly goes as late as 1am.
