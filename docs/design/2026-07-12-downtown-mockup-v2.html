Good to Know

What do I wear?

Turf shoes or flat-soled trainers and shinguards. No metal studs.

How big is a 6v6 squad?

Six on the field; rosters usually carry 9–10 so you can rotate. Register solo and we'll place you.

Is there parking?

Free lot right at the building on Starr Ave. (owner confirm)

Can people watch?

Yes — spectator seating and standing room along the field.

Is it hard to find?

It's tucked in a quiet pocket — look for the long white building between Gray St and Loew St, entrance on the Starr Ave side. Full directions below.

How late are you open?

4pm to midnight, every day.
