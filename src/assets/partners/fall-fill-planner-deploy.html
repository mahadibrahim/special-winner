Read this before trusting the numbers: inputs are conservative planning priors, replaced by observed per-channel data (tracked links + discount codes) at two-week checkpoints. Fall 2026 is the cold start; later seasons add renewals, the email list, seasonal demand, and compounding awareness — that is where team counts grow.

ROAS counts registration revenue only. Staff hours are estimates. Print costs are planning estimates from typical online bulk pricing (yard signs ~$8–12/unit at qty 50, flyers ~$0.15 at qty 1,000) — get vendor quotes before committing. Giveaway prizes and free field hours appear as forgone value, not cash — capacity that would mostly go unsold. WhatsApp broadcasts go only to explicitly opted-in contacts (WABA requirement), costed at marketing-conversation rates. Paid ads: $2,000/month cap for the first six months of operation, $1,000/month after (decided 2026-07-11), hard-enforced at the payment card, 3:1 scale rule. The Founding Ten giveaway runs in Fall 2026 only. New-audience acquisition is damped by a finite addressable-player pool (the demand ceiling) and awareness plateaus. Futsal pricing is a planning assumption — no futsal offer is priced yet. The capacity meter tracks soccer fields only; futsal teams use the separate court capacity. Leagues operated by Aspire Sports.
