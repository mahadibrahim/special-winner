# Venue Manager Command Center — Narration Script

Source video: `training/output/venue-manager/video.webm`

1. [00:06] Welcome to the venue command center — your run-of-show for everything happening today.
2. [00:10] This is the check-in station, where players and teams check in as they arrive.
3. [00:12] If a family walks up without registering ahead of time, this is where you sign them up on the spot — waiver, payment, and roster in one form.
4. [00:17] Over on Games, you'll find every scheduled match across every season.
5. [00:25] Open a match's Refs panel to see who's assigned, their fee, and whether they've been paid — this is also where payroll hand-off gets logged.
6. [00:26] Media Shoots lists every photo and video assignment for the day.
7. [00:33] Open a shoot to see its check-in status — that's the signature that starts the photographer's pay clock once they sign in on site.
8. [00:34] Once the day wraps up, End-of-Day Reports gives you the full summary.
